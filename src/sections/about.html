The organization was founded on a simple belief:

When good forces unite — a powerful system is created, capable of changing reality for the better. Our role is to serve as a bridge connecting organizations, nonprofits, initiatives, and groups — to create a space for collaboration and to amplify cumulative impact through the power of community and social connection.

We work to connect people and organizations acting for society, to make information accessible, to encourage collaborative dialogue, and to build an infrastructure of mutual recognition, trust, and joint action.
