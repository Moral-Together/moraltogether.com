Our Story

The organization was founded on a simple belief:

When good forces unite — a powerful system is created, capable of changing reality for the better. Our role is to serve as a bridge connecting organizations, nonprofits, initiatives, and groups — to create a space for collaboration and to amplify cumulative impact through the power of community and social connection.

We work to connect people and organizations acting for society, to make information accessible, to encourage collaborative dialogue, and to build an infrastructure of mutual recognition, trust, and joint action.

Our Overarching Goal

Creating economic, security, health, nutritional, and social resilience for a united, healthy, and developing society. To put that belief, role, action, and these goals into practice, we have established social systems that touch every circle of life.

Communications

We have communications studios equipped to the highest technological standard, serving as a voice and a stage for positive social communication for all ages.

A Comprehensive Social Media Network

At the core of our activity is turning the digital space into a space that spreads good and connects people to it. We see digital as an enormous opportunity for positive impact. In the place where people are every day, we seek to give a stage to good deeds, to initiatives of value, to organizations working for society, and to messages that strengthen hope, mutual responsibility, and connection worth making.

Through this platform we centralize and make accessible the good that already exists, connect people with opportunities for action, and enable positive content to reach wider audiences — turning exposure into involvement, and involvement into action.

The goal is not only to tell about the good — but to use the power of digital to spread the good, connect more people to it, and increase its impact in the real world.

Our Vision

We believe that when good people connect — good things happen.

MoralTogether connects people, communities, businesses, organizations, and academia, to create collaborations that bring good ideas into action and assured impact.

We create a place where you can get to know one another, share knowledge and experience, find the right people, and connect whoever has an idea, a need, or a resource — with whoever can help turn it into something bigger.

Our goal is simple: to unite forces, to open opportunities, and to turn goodwill into action that advances us all together.
